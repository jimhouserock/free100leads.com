# Free100Leads

Free100Leads hands out 100 B2B company leads a day at no cost and without requiring an account to start browsing.

## Filtering leads

Visitors narrow the list by country, US state, industry, and company size to get leads that match a specific target market.

## Daily quota and bonus spin

Once the 100 free leads for the day are used, a bonus wheel offers a chance at extra leads. Leads viewed without an account disappear when the tab closes.

## Saving leads

Creating a free account keeps whatever leads were pulled that day instead of losing them.

## Paid plans

The free tier is view-only, no export and no API. Paid plans add higher daily volume, CSV export, bulk download, and a metered API for pulling leads programmatically.
